## Scope

This offer covers the corresponding source of: RetroArch (GPLv3) and the
libretro cores fceumm, gambatte, mupen64plus_next, pcsx_rearmed, desmume
(GPLv2), bsnes, blastem, gearsystem (GPLv3), and the MPL-2.0 covered
files of the mgba core. The EmulationStation engine (MIT) imposes no such
obligation, but its source is available at the upstream listed in
`THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`.
